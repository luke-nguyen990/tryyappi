Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 19, 2026 | Effective Date: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Yappi ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our website at tryyappi.com, our Shopify application ("Yappi AI Sales Chatbot & Instant FAQ"), and related services (collectively, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

When you install and use Yappi via Shopify, we collect the necessary information required to provide autonomous customer service and sales assistance:

  • Merchant Information: Store name, primary contact email, domain URL, and Shopify shop ID.
  • Store Catalog Data: Public product titles, descriptions, pricing, inventory availability, and store policies (shipping, returns, FAQs).
  • Customer Interaction Data: Messages and queries sent by storefront shoppers to the AI assistant to facilitate real-time answers and order tracking.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Provide, maintain, and optimize the AI chatbot responses for your store.
  • Synchronize catalog data for accurate product recommendations.
  • Perform order status lookups requested directly by verified store shoppers.
  • Provide customer support, critical security notices, and feature updates.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest) to protect all merchant and customer data. We do not sell, rent, or trade merchant or customer data to third-party advertisers.

4. Compliance & User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

In accordance with GDPR and CCPA regulations, merchants and their customers have the right to request access to, correction of, or complete deletion of their personal data. Automated Shopify customer data erasure webhooks are processed within 48 hours of receipt.
